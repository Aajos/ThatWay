ThatWay Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]. Applies to the ThatWay iPhone app and its server.

ThatWay is a compass that can point you to a friend, but only if that friend agrees. This page says what we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it. We wrote it to match what the app actually does.

The short version

  • Your location is shared with a friend only while you have allowed that friend to trace you, and only for the time you chose (15 minutes to 24 hours). You can stop it at any moment.
  • We store only your latest shared position, and it is deleted within 2 hours at most, and straight away when a trace ends.
  • The person tracing you never has their own location uploaded. Only a rounded distance band is sent when they look.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no data sold, and no third-party SDKs in the app.
  • You can delete your account and data inside the app.

What stays on your phone

  • The compass, heading, and your own location when you are not being traced are used on your phone and not sent to us.
  • Preferences and the compass theme are stored on your phone.
  • Your sign-in is stored in the iOS Keychain.

Location in the background

While you have allowed at least one friend to trace you, ThatWay keeps sharing your location with that friend even when the app is in the background. iOS shows its blue location indicator while this happens. It stops when the permission ends, when you stop it, or if you swipe ThatWay away completely. ThatWay needs "While Using the App" and Precise Location to do this. It does not need "Always".

We also use your location, without sharing it, to point the compass, find places near you, and guide you on a trip. During a trip guided by the app, location keeps working with the screen locked.

Camera

If you tap "Show direction" when finding a friend up close, ThatWay turns on the camera so the phone can work out which way to point. The camera is used only for that, only while it is on. ThatWay does not record, store or send camera images.

Who else receives information

  • Your friend, as described above: only what the trace permission covers.
  • Amazon Web Services hosts our servers, sign-in and database, in Sydney, Australia.
  • Apple provides maps and place search (MapKit), push notifications, and purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies to what Apple handles.
  • A route-planning service. To draw a route, the app sends the start and end points of that route to a public routing service based on OpenStreetMap data, currently operated by FOSSGIS e.V. in Germany. It does not receive your name, email or account. [UPDATE THIS PARAGRAPH if the routing provider changes before launch.]

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety; where we can, we will tell the person concerned.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop a trace any time: the banner in the app, or the You tab, with no confirmation needed.
  • Block or report a person from their profile or the You tab.
  • Deactivate your account: your friends and traces are removed and you are hidden from search; your sign-in and plan are kept and the account is deleted automatically after 12 months unless you come back.
  • Delete everything in the app (You tab): your friendships, requests, permissions, position, blocks, profile and sign-in are removed. Reports and purchase identifiers are kept as stated above.
  • Ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or to correct it, at [CONTACT EMAIL].
  • You can turn off location, camera or notifications for ThatWay in iOS Settings at any time. Some features then stop working, and the app tells you which.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Allowing a trace needs the phone's owner to confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or the phone passcode (or a ThatWay passcode on a phone with no lock). No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise it.

Children

ThatWay is not for anyone under [16]. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Safety notice

ThatWay is not an emergency service and cannot send help. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number.

Changes

If we change how we use your information in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies, and ask for your agreement again where a trace permission depends on it. The date at the top shows the latest version.
